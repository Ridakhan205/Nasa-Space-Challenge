AI model training & live telemetry

A lightweight personalized anomaly model that runs entirely in your browser. It learns a robust reference profile from CSV readings or demo data, then scores each incoming observation.

1 · Train personal baseline

CSV columns: hr, hrv, sbp, dbp, spo2 (at least two, 10+ rows). Common names like heart_rate, rmssd, systolic and diastolic are recognised, and commas, semicolons or tabs all work. Training starts automatically when you pick a file.
